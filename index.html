The first macro-level, data-driven view of how early modern English poetry circulated in manuscript. I lead Work Package 3, which models that circulation as a network to find the literary communities behind it.

About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for Data and AI Research, University of Galway. I argue that web engineering, sustainability and accessibility have to be tackled together if the web is to serve everyone without an outsized environmental cost.

Since July 2026 my postdoc has focused on STEMMA, a European Research Council Consolidator project led by Prof. Erin A. McCarthy, where I lead Work Package 3: modelling how early modern English manuscript poetry circulated as a network. Before that I worked on the EU-funded Data Spaces Support Centre (DSSC), built GreenWebInspector for real-time web energy profiling, and lead a research line on digital equity for rare-disease patient communities. Before that I completed my PhD and MS at LUMS on context-aware sports analytics, web energy optimisation and program analysis, and my BS at NUST in the High-Performance Computing Lab.

Research programme

Four connected themes

Each theme pairs measurement with tools that under-resourced organisations can actually use.

01

Green Web & Carbon-Aware Design

Methods and tools for measuring, modelling and reducing the energy footprint of web applications, from static design features to live profiling, with next steps towards energy regression testing in CI/CD.

Web energyCarbon-aware designChrome extensions
02

Web Accessibility & Universal Design

The WARD audit covered 103 rare-disease patient organisation websites in five countries and found none fully compliant. I am now building automated and crowdsourced remediation for organisations without accessibility teams.

WCAGUniversal designDigital health equity
03

Data Spaces & Interoperability

Through the EU Data Spaces Support Centre, I work on design principles for trustworthy, FAIR data sharing across European sectors, with a focus on governance, energy and accessibility.

DSSCFAIR dataGovernance
04

Computational Humanities & Networks

On the ERC Consolidator project STEMMA I lead the work package that turns six datasets of early modern English verse manuscripts into a network, tracing how poems moved between readers, scribes and communities from 1475 to 1700.

ERC STEMMANetwork analysisManuscripts
